Fixed Asset Register Updater

Upload your existing FAR, this period's Fixed Asset GL entries, and any invoices — get back an updated FAR workbook with new additions and rolled-forward depreciation. Your original file is never changed.

Required files

The exact FAR file you already maintain, with the "FAR Schedule" sheet and category sheets.

One row per addition: date, a unique voucher/reference, the GL account/category, and the amount.

Invoices for new additions (optional, but recommended)

Columns: invoice number, invoice date, vendor, description, amount, and optionally category/reference. This is the most reliable way to feed invoice data in.

Best-effort text extraction only (no OCR/handwriting recognition). Scanned invoices with no extractable text are treated as "no invoice" and flagged for review.

Period details

The "as of" date for this update run.

1 for monthly, 3 for quarterly, etc.

Leave blank to auto-detect from the FAR Schedule sheet's title (e.g. "AS ON JUNE 30, 2025").

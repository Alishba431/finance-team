Balance Sheet Audit Trail

v1 · built 30 Jul 2026

Upload a workbook with SOFP, Trial Balance and General Ledger sheets and get an automated audit trail: every Balance Sheet line is traced to its Trial Balance accounts and General Ledger transactions, reconciled against the reported figure, with differences and missing mappings clearly flagged.

Bring
  • An Excel workbook (.xlsx/.xlsm) with at least three sheets: a Statement of Financial Position (SOFP) with a year header row, a Trial Balance with Debit/Credit or Closing Balance columns, and a General Ledger with one block of dated transactions per account (any sheet names are fine, they're confirmed/selected after upload)

Does not Supplementary note sheets referenced via a 'Note' column on the SOFP are not cross-referenced automatically; Formula errors in the source workbook (e.g. #REF!) are flagged rather than guessed at

Tap a tool to open it. Each one runs on your machine and takes your real data at the moment you use it — none of it is stored here, and none of it was given to the builder.

SI Subscription Invoice Fetcher Upload your subscription invoice breakdown sheet, connect the admin Gmail inbox once, and get back the same sheet with a Status per row (Found, Found - No Attachment, Not Found, or Missing Data) plus a link to the saved invoice attachment for anything the tool located automatically.
BS Balance Sheet Audit Trail Traces every line on the Statement of Financial Position back to its supporting Trial Balance accounts and General Ledger transactions, flagging anything that doesn't reconcile. Data can come from an Excel upload (SOFP/TB/GL) or a direct read-only pull from the connected staging Odoo instance for a chosen date range.
FA Fixed Asset Register Auto-Updater Upload your Fixed Asset Register once as a baseline, then each period upload that period's invoices and GL; the tool files new purchases into the correct existing asset tab, calculates depreciation the same way the register already does, applies disposals to the matching existing row, and lists anything it couldn't confidently resolve for you to handle by hand.

FA Fixed Asset Register Updater Upload your existing Fixed Asset Register, this period's Fixed Asset GL entries, and any invoices you have, and get back an updated FAR workbook: new additions are added without duplicates, and depreciation, accumulated depreciation and NBV are rolled forward for every asset - old and new - for the period. A Needs Review sheet flags anything that needs a human look (missing invoices, unmatched GL rows, or category totals that don't tie to the GL). Your original FAR file is never changed.
BS Balance Sheet Audit Trail Upload a workbook with SOFP, Trial Balance and General Ledger sheets and get a line-by-line audit trail showing how each Statement of Financial Position figure ties back to its supporting TB accounts and GL transactions, with unmapped accounts flagged for you to assign.
